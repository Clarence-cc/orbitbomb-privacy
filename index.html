Icefire Interactive · Orbit Bomb

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Icefire Interactive ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use Orbit Bomb, our Android game (package name: com.icefireinteractive.orbitbomb).

1. Information handled by the game

Orbit Bomb does not require an account. Game progress, scores, coins, lives, unlocked satellites, reward records, and settings are stored locally on your device. We do not operate a server to receive these game saves. Device backup services may handle app data according to your device settings and the service provider's terms.

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address, your message, and any information or attachments you choose to send. We use this information to answer your request and provide support. Please do not send unnecessary sensitive information.

2. Advertising and third-party services

Orbit Bomb uses Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK) to provide advertising, including optional rewarded ads that can grant an in-game life. The SDK collects and shares information with Google for ad delivery, advertising measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention. Depending on your device, applicable settings, and privacy choices, this includes:

  • Your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location.
  • Device and account identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID, and other identifiers where applicable.
  • App and advertising interactions, such as app launches, taps, and video views.
  • Diagnostic and performance information about the app and advertising SDK.

Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) is used to collect and store privacy choices where required. Google and the advertising partners disclosed in the consent message may process information to deliver and measure ads in accordance with those choices and their respective policies.

Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from apps that use its services, and Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosures.

3. Your advertising and privacy choices

Where required, a privacy message allows you to review advertising partners and accept, reject, or manage relevant data uses. Where available, you can reopen these choices using PRIVACY in the game's settings. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that occurred before withdrawal. Your choices may affect the ads available to you.

You can also manage, reset, or delete your advertising ID in Android settings, depending on your Android version. Choosing to watch a rewarded ad is separate from giving consent to personalized advertising.

4. How and why information is used and shared

Local information supports gameplay, saves your progress, and records rewards. Support correspondence is used to respond to you. Advertising information supports the purposes described above. We do not sell your local game saves or support correspondence. Advertising data is processed by Google and relevant advertising partners as described in their disclosures.

Where applicable data protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on consent for processing that requires consent, and on our legitimate interests in providing support, operating the game, and maintaining security for other necessary processing, subject to your rights. We may retain or disclose information when required by law or necessary to address fraud, security, or legal claims.

5. Retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until it is removed. You can delete it through Android's app storage settings by clearing the game's data. Uninstalling normally removes local app data, but backups may remain or later restore it; manage those separately through your backup provider. Deleting local data will erase your saved progress.

We retain support correspondence only as long as needed to resolve your request and meet necessary security or legal obligations. You may email us to request its deletion. Data collected by Google and advertising partners is retained under their respective policies; deleting the game does not automatically delete information already held by them. See Google's data retention information and privacy controls for details.

6. Security and international processing

We take reasonable steps to protect information we handle. Google states that the Mobile Ads SDK encrypts transmitted user data using TLS. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security. Google and other service providers may process information outside your country, subject to their applicable safeguards and policies.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of personal information, receive a portable copy, or withdraw consent. You may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority. Contact us using the email below for requests about information we hold. For information controlled by Google or another provider, their privacy policies explain the available request mechanisms. We cannot identify or retrieve your local game save remotely.

8. Children's privacy

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review the concern and take appropriate action. Do not send a child's sensitive information in your initial request.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game or its data practices change. Updates will appear on this page with a revised date. We will provide additional notice or request consent where required.
