Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Icefire Interactive ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use Orbit Bomb, our Android game (package name: com.icefireinteractive.orbitbomb).

Terms of Service · Accounts and leaderboards · Delete your data · Contact us

1. Information handled by the game

The current version of Orbit Bomb does not provide account registration, Google Play Games sign-in, or online leaderboards. Progress, local best scores and stages, coins, lives, unlocked darts, boss collection records, rewards, and settings are stored on your device. We do not operate our own server for these game saves or provide an in-game cloud-save service. Device backup services may handle app data according to your device settings and the provider's terms.

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address, your message, and any information or attachments you choose to send. We use this information to answer your request and provide support. Please do not send unnecessary sensitive information.

2. Advertising and third-party services

Crash reports. On Android versions that include Firebase Crashlytics, crash reporting starts automatically. Google processes reports for Icefire Interactive so we can diagnose faults and measure how many installations they affect. Reports can include crash stack traces, Unity script exceptions, native crash dumps, the game version, device and operating system details, installation identifiers, and the level and game phase at the time of the issue. Analytics events can also supply a short history of actions leading up to the issue. A report may be sent when the game next starts. The game has no crash-reporting switch.

Gameplay analytics. Orbit Bomb automatically collects gameplay analytics on Android. This collection is enabled at startup and is required when using this version of the game, including after an update from a version with an optional analytics setting. Google Analytics for Firebase receives level numbers, outcomes, and revives, along with an app instance identifier, app and device information, and automatic usage events such as sessions. We use these data to understand player activity, retention, and level difficulty. Google may derive an approximate region from the connection IP address. We disable advertising ID collection for Firebase Analytics. Event data follows the Analytics property's retention settings. Advertising privacy controls are described below.

Orbit Bomb uses Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK) to provide advertising, including optional rewarded ads that can grant an in-game life, let you continue a run, or contribute toward unlocking a dart. At the end of a run, the game may also display a full-screen interstitial ad. At startup the game requests a public configuration file to check whether these ads are enabled and how often to show them. The hosting provider receives the network request, including your IP address. The SDK collects and shares information with Google for ad delivery, advertising measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention. Depending on your device, applicable settings, and privacy choices, this includes:

  • Your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location.
  • Device and account identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID, and other identifiers where applicable.
  • App and advertising interactions, such as app launches, taps, and video views.
  • Diagnostic and performance information about the app and advertising SDK.

Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) is used to collect and store privacy choices where required. Google and the advertising partners disclosed in the consent message may process information to deliver and measure ads in accordance with those choices and their respective policies.

Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from apps that use its services, and Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosures.

3. Accounts, leaderboards, and earlier versions

The current version does not include the Google Play Games SDK. It does not automatically sign you in to Play Games, read your Play Games player identifier, display name or avatar, or submit scores or stages to Google leaderboards. Best scores and stages remain local. Any pending leaderboard records left on your device by an earlier version are not read, retried, or uploaded by this version.

If you used an earlier version or test build with Google Play Games enabled, Google may still hold your previously submitted scores, stages, and associated gamer profile information. Updating the game does not automatically delete those records. Section 6 explains how to manage them. This is separate from the Google advertising services that remain in the current version.

4. Your advertising and privacy choices

Where required, a privacy message allows you to review advertising partners and accept, reject, or manage relevant data uses. Where available, you can reopen these choices using PRIVACY in the game's settings. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that occurred before withdrawal. Your choices may affect the ads available to you.

You can also manage, reset, or delete your advertising ID in Android settings, depending on your Android version. Choosing to watch a rewarded ad is separate from giving consent to personalized advertising.

5. How and why information is used and shared

Local information supports gameplay, saves your progress, and records rewards. Support correspondence is used to respond to you. Advertising information supports the purposes described above. We do not sell your local game saves or support correspondence. Advertising data is processed by Google and relevant advertising partners as described in their disclosures.

Where applicable data protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on consent for processing that requires consent, and on our legitimate interests in providing support, operating the game, and maintaining security for other necessary processing, subject to your rights. We may retain or disclose information when required by law or necessary to address fraud, security, or legal claims.

6. Retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until it is removed. You can delete it through Android's app storage settings by clearing the game's data. Uninstalling normally removes local app data, but backups may remain or later restore it; manage those separately through your backup provider. Deleting local data will erase your saved progress.

Records from earlier Play Games versions: if you previously used a version with Play Games enabled, clearing local app data or uninstalling Orbit Bomb does not by itself remove records already held by Google. To delete data for this game, follow Google's instructions for deleting Play Games data or your Play Games profile and select Orbit Bomb where a per-game option is available. You do not need to delete your entire Google account.

Firebase temporarily processes native crash dumps. Google retains crash traces, data extracted from dumps, and associated installation identifiers for 90 days before beginning removal from its live and backup systems. For details, see Firebase privacy and retention information.

Earlier local pending submissions: this version does not process or upload them. They may remain in local app storage or device backups. Clearing app storage removes them together with your local progress; manage backups separately if you want to remove backed-up copies. You do not need to erase your save just to stop leaderboard uploads in this version.

Requests to Icefire Interactive: email icefireinteractive@gmail.com with the subject "Orbit Bomb data deletion request" to request deletion of support correspondence or ask for help locating the relevant deletion controls. Identify the correspondence you want removed; do not send passwords, identity documents, or other unnecessary sensitive information. We can handle information we hold, but cannot directly delete your Google account or retrieve your local save from a server.

We retain support correspondence only as long as needed to resolve your request and meet necessary security or legal obligations. You may email us to request its deletion. Data collected by Google and advertising partners is retained under their respective policies; deleting the game does not automatically delete information already held by them. See Google's data retention information and privacy controls for details.

7. Security and international processing

We take reasonable steps to protect information we handle. Google states that the Mobile Ads SDK encrypts its collected user data in transit using TLS. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security. Google and other service providers may process information outside your country, subject to their applicable safeguards and policies.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of personal information, receive a portable copy, or withdraw consent. You may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority. Contact us using the email below for requests about information we hold. For information controlled by Google or another provider, their privacy policies explain the available request mechanisms. We cannot identify or retrieve your local game save remotely.

9. Age and children's privacy

Orbit Bomb is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. This intended audience does not replace the age or consent requirements that apply in your country or to your Google account. The game does not currently ask you to enter a date of birth.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review the concern and take appropriate action. Do not send a child's sensitive information in your initial request.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game or its data practices change. Updates will appear on this page with a revised date. We will provide additional notice or request consent where required.
