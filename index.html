Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Icefire Interactive ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use Orbit Bomb, our Android game (package name: com.icefireinteractive.orbitbomb).

Play Games and leaderboards · Delete your data · Contact us

1. Information handled by the game

You can play Orbit Bomb without signing in to Google Play Games or creating an account with Icefire Interactive. Progress, local best scores and stages, coins, lives, unlocked darts, boss collection records, rewards, and settings are stored on your device. We do not operate our own server for these game saves or provide an in-game cloud-save service. Google Play Games leaderboard records are handled separately as described below. Device backup services may handle app data according to your device settings and the provider's terms.

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address, your message, and any information or attachments you choose to send. We use this information to answer your request and provide support. Please do not send unnecessary sensitive information.

2. Advertising and third-party services

Orbit Bomb uses Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK) to provide advertising, including optional rewarded ads that can grant an in-game life, let you continue a run, or contribute toward unlocking a dart. The SDK collects and shares information with Google for ad delivery, advertising measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention. Depending on your device, applicable settings, and privacy choices, this includes:

  • Your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location.
  • Device and account identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID, and other identifiers where applicable.
  • App and advertising interactions, such as app launches, taps, and video views.
  • Diagnostic and performance information about the app and advertising SDK.

Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) is used to collect and store privacy choices where required. Google and the advertising partners disclosed in the consent message may process information to deliver and measure ads in accordance with those choices and their respective policies.

Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from apps that use its services, and Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosures.

3. Google Play Games and leaderboards

Orbit Bomb uses Google Play Games Services for platform sign-in and two leaderboards: Stage and Score. The game attempts automatic sign-in at startup using your Play Games settings. Selecting a board from RANK can start an interactive sign-in or profile setup flow if needed. Signing in is not required for the core game.

When signed in, the game uses your Play Games player identifier to associate submissions with the correct player. The Play Games SDK handles your gamer profile, including your display name and avatar; the native Google leaderboard can show your profile and ranking according to your Google profile visibility settings. The game does not request your Google password, an email-access permission, or access to your contacts or friends list.

The Stage board receives stages you reach during a tracked run. The Score board receives your final run score when the run ends, including any continuation. Google stores and compares these values to provide rankings. The game keeps failed submissions locally under the associated player identifier and retries them; pending values are removed locally after a confirmed successful upload. Historical runs completed entirely without sign-in are not automatically imported into a later account. Startup sign-in that finishes during a run can associate that active run with the signed-in player.

These leaderboards do not back up your coins, lives, dart collection, or full game save. Google's Play Games services also process service usage and diagnostic information. See Google's Play Games data disclosures and Play Games profile privacy and sign-in settings. You can manage automatic sign-in and profile visibility using Google's settings; these controls are separate from advertising privacy choices.

4. Your advertising and privacy choices

Where required, a privacy message allows you to review advertising partners and accept, reject, or manage relevant data uses. Where available, you can reopen these choices using PRIVACY in the game's settings. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that occurred before withdrawal. Your choices may affect the ads available to you.

You can also manage, reset, or delete your advertising ID in Android settings, depending on your Android version. Choosing to watch a rewarded ad is separate from giving consent to personalized advertising.

5. How and why information is used and shared

Local information supports gameplay, saves your progress, and records rewards. Support correspondence is used to respond to you. Advertising information supports the purposes described above. Play Games player identifiers and gameplay records support sign-in, account association, and rankings. Display names, avatars, and leaderboard records may be visible to other players according to your Google profile settings. We do not sell your local game saves or support correspondence. Advertising data is processed by Google and relevant advertising partners as described in their disclosures.

Where applicable data protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on consent for processing that requires consent, and on our legitimate interests in providing support, operating the game, and maintaining security for other necessary processing, subject to your rights. We may retain or disclose information when required by law or necessary to address fraud, security, or legal claims.

6. Retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until it is removed. You can delete it through Android's app storage settings by clearing the game's data. Uninstalling normally removes local app data, but backups may remain or later restore it; manage those separately through your backup provider. Deleting local data will erase your saved progress.

Google Play Games records: clearing local app data or uninstalling Orbit Bomb does not by itself remove records already held by Google. To delete data for this game, follow Google's instructions for deleting Play Games data or your Play Games profile and select Orbit Bomb where a per-game option is available. You do not need to delete your entire Google account.

Pending submissions and future play: local pending leaderboard submissions can remain until uploaded or local app data is cleared. If you also want to remove these pending values, close the game and clear its app storage, noting that this deletes local progress too. Manage automatic sign-in before playing again if you do not want new signed-in gameplay to create new leaderboard records. Backups may restore local pending data, so manage those separately when deleting data.

Requests to Icefire Interactive: email icefireinteractive@gmail.com with the subject "Orbit Bomb data deletion request" to request deletion of support correspondence or ask for help locating the relevant deletion controls. Identify the correspondence you want removed; do not send passwords, identity documents, or other unnecessary sensitive information. We can handle information we hold, but cannot directly delete your Google account or retrieve your local save from a server.

We retain support correspondence only as long as needed to resolve your request and meet necessary security or legal obligations. You may email us to request its deletion. Data collected by Google and advertising partners is retained under their respective policies; deleting the game does not automatically delete information already held by them. See Google's data retention information and privacy controls for details.

7. Security and international processing

We take reasonable steps to protect information we handle. Google states that the Mobile Ads SDK uses TLS and Play Games Services uses HTTPS to protect the SDK data described in their disclosures while it is transmitted. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security. Google and other service providers may process information outside your country, subject to their applicable safeguards and policies.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of personal information, receive a portable copy, or withdraw consent. You may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority. Contact us using the email below for requests about information we hold. For information controlled by Google or another provider, their privacy policies explain the available request mechanisms. We cannot identify or retrieve your local game save remotely.

9. Age and children's privacy

Orbit Bomb is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. This intended audience does not replace the age or consent requirements that apply in your country or to your Google account. The game does not currently ask you to enter a date of birth.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review the concern and take appropriate action. Do not send a child's sensitive information in your initial request.
